What is llms.txt?


llms.txt is a simple text file added to your website root that tells AI systems (like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) what your site is about, which pages are important and your AI usage permissions. Just like robots.txt for search engines, llms.txt is a guide for AI engines.


As of 2026, llms.txt has become a cornerstone of GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) strategy. Introducing yourself correctly to AI systems significantly boosts your chances of being cited.



Why is llms.txt Important?


Introduce Yourself to AI


Without llms.txt, AI has to guess about your site. With this file you clearly state your brand, services and important pages.


Higher Chance of Being Cited


AI finds sites that introduce themselves clearly more trustworthy, increasing the likelihood of being cited.


State Your AI Usage Permissions


You decide how your content can be used and how it should be cited.
