Is Your Site Visible
on ChatGPT? 2026 AI SEO Guide

📅 June 8, 2026 ✍️ Scanliq Team ⏱️ 7 min read

By 2026, a significant portion of web traffic comes from AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overview. Yet most website owners still haven't asked the key question: "Is AI even aware my site exists?"

Important: The fundamentals of traditional SEO and AI SEO are similar, but AI systems evaluate content differently. This guide covers the differences and exactly what you need to do.

How Do AI Search Engines Work?

When ChatGPT answers a question, it either browses the web in real time or refers to sites in its training data. Perplexity relies entirely on real-time web browsing. Both filter sources based on specific criteria.

2. Write Strong Meta Descriptions

AI systems use meta descriptions as page summaries. Write clear, keyword-rich descriptions between 120-160 characters.

3. Add Schema.org Structured Data

Structured data tells AI what your content is about in machine-readable language. See our Schema.org guide for step-by-step instructions.

4. Write Content in Q&A Format

AI systems scan sites to answer questions. Organize your content around formats like "What is X?", "How to do X?" and add FAQ sections.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which sites does ChatGPT recommend?
ChatGPT prefers sites that use HTTPS, have a meta description, contain Schema.org structured data, and haven't blocked GPTBot in robots.txt.
Should I block GPTBot?
If you want your site to be recommended by ChatGPT, it's advised not to block GPTBot. If you block it, ChatGPT won't show your site as a source.
Is AI SEO the same as classic SEO?
The fundamentals are similar, but AI SEO requires more structured data, clear content organization, and AI-specific permission files like llms.txt.
