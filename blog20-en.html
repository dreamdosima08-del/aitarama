What is AEO? Answer Engine Optimization

📅 July 1, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 7 min read

You've heard of GEO, you already know SEO — but what about AEO? Three-letter acronyms have multiplied in the AI search world and the confusion has grown. This guide clearly explains what AEO is, how it differs from GEO, and why you should think about both together.

AEO in one sentence: AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring your content so AI shows it as a direct answer — in Google AI Overviews, featured snippets, voice assistants and Copilot.

What is AEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring your content to directly and clearly answer a question. The goal: when a user asks a question, your content gets selected as the "featured answer." This happens in Google AI Overviews, featured snippets, "People Also Ask" boxes, Bing Copilot and voice assistants.

AEO isn't actually a new concept — it emerged when Google started showing featured snippets. But it became critical in the AI era because most searches now end without a click.

The Difference Between AEO and GEO

These two are often confused, and some experts even argue they're the same thing. But there's a technical difference:

CriterionAEOGEO
What it targetsDirect answer / snippetCitation in the AI response
Simple phrasing"Give me the best sentence""Give the best info to build a response"
How content is usedYour sentence is copied exactlyYour content is synthesized/rewritten
Where it appearsSearch page, snippet, voiceChat window, AI summary
Example platformGoogle AI Overview, Copilot, SiriChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude

In short: AEO focuses on appearing in the direct answer box, GEO on being cited as a source in the AI-generated response. We drew the big picture of this trio in our GEO vs SEO post.

How to Structure Content for AEO?

1. Answer-First Structure

A page's first sentence should completely answer the main question. Answer engines look for that quick validation. Avoid long intros.

2. Question-Answer Format

FAQ blocks are perfect for "People Also Ask." Turn real questions into headings and answer clearly.

3. Structure-Friendly Format

Lists, tables, bullet points — use formats that align with how answer engines present information.

4. Machine-Readable Language

Use clear, precise, plain language. Ambiguity and unnecessary jargon reduce your content's extractability. Structured data (Schema) strengthens this.

AEO, GEO and SEO: Think of All Three Together

The expert consensus is clear: these three aren't competitors, they're layers. SEO provides baseline visibility, AEO gets you into direct answers, and GEO gets you cited in AI-generated responses. A modern strategy should address all three. Our AI SEO checklist covers this holistic approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are AEO and GEO the same thing?
They're very close and some experts argue they're the same. The technical difference: AEO focuses on appearing in the direct answer box (snippet, AI Overview), while GEO focuses on being cited as a source in the AI-generated response.
What does AEO stand for?
AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It's the practice of structuring your content so AI shows it as a direct answer.
What's the most important step for AEO?
Answer-first structure is most critical: the page's first sentence should clearly and completely answer the main question. FAQ blocks and clear, extractable language also matter.
