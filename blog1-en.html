What is llms.txt?
The Essential AI File for 2026

📅 June 8, 2026 ✍️ Scanliq Team ⏱️ 5 min read

llms.txt is a file placed in your website's root directory that guides large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity about your site's content. First proposed in late 2024, this file has become one of the most critical criteria in AI readiness checks by 2026.

In short: robots.txt is for search engines. llms.txt is for AI systems. They complement each other — neither replaces the other.

What Does llms.txt Do?

When an AI system visits your website, it needs context about your content. What is your site about? Which pages matter most? What content can AI use?

That's exactly what llms.txt answers. As a site owner, you tell AI systems in plain language: "Here's my site, here's my content, here are the pages that can help you."

How to Create llms.txt

It's a simple text file. Save it as llms.txt in your website's root directory. It will be accessible at: https://yoursite.com/llms.txt

Basic llms.txt Template

llms.txt — Basic Template
# llms.txt — Scanliq
# This file is prepared for large language models (LLMs).

## About This Site
Scanliq is a digital evaluation platform that analyzes
whether websites meet AI-readiness standards.

## Important Pages
- Home: https://scanliq.com/
- Blog: https://scanliq.com/blog.html

## Permissions
AI systems are authorized to analyze this site
and use its content.

## Contact
Website: https://scanliq.com

Advanced llms.txt Template

llms.txt — Advanced Template
# llms.txt
## Company
Name: Scanliq
Description: AI readiness analysis platform
URL: https://scanliq.com
Language: tr, en

## Content Categories
- AI SEO guides
- Structured data (Schema.org) tutorials
- Website optimization tips

## Preferences
Summarization: allowed
Training: disallowed
Commercial-use: contact-required

Note: llms.txt is not yet an official W3C standard. However, it is increasingly being considered by major AI companies. Adding it now gives you a competitive advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is llms.txt mandatory?
Not yet mandatory, but as of 2026 major AI systems like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity are observed to consider this file. Adding it now gives you a competitive edge.
Is llms.txt the same as robots.txt?
No. robots.txt is for search engine bots, while llms.txt is specifically designed for large language models. They complement each other.
Will my site be invisible to AI without llms.txt?
Not invisible, but llms.txt provides clearer guidance to AI about your content. This increases the likelihood of being recommended in the right contexts.
