How to Appear in Bing Copilot?

📅 June 22, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 6 min read

When people think of AI visibility, ChatGPT and Google come to mind. But Microsoft Copilot — embedded in Windows, Office and the Edge browser — is a massive AI assistant used by millions every day. And most site owners ignore it completely. This guide covers everything you need to know to get your site seen and cited by Bing Copilot.

Why it matters: Copilot uses the Bing search infrastructure. So your visibility on Bing directly determines your chance of being cited in Copilot answers. Ignoring Bing means missing a huge AI audience.

How Does Microsoft Copilot Work?

When a user asks a question, Copilot pulls relevant sources from Bing's search index, synthesizes them with AI, and produces an answer with source links. That's why the foundation of Copilot visibility is being properly crawled and indexed by Bing.

bingbot: The Gateway to Copilot

Bing's crawler is bingbot. If your robots.txt blocks bingbot, your site won't be indexed on Bing — so Copilot can never cite you. The first step is making sure bingbot access is open.

Check bingbot Access

Your robots.txt shouldn't have a Disallow: / rule for bingbot. We explained how in our robots.txt check guide.

5 Signals for Copilot Readiness

Scanliq calculates a "Copilot Readiness" score based on five core signals:

1. HTTPS

A secure connection is Bing's baseline expectation. HTTP sites are at a serious disadvantage.

2. bingbot Access

robots.txt must not block bingbot — this is non-negotiable.

3. sitemap.xml

Bing uses your sitemap to discover pages. Combined with llms.txt, it strengthens discoverability.

4. Canonical and Meta Description

Bing relies on the canonical tag to avoid duplicate content and on meta description for summarization.

5. Structured Data

Schema.org or JSON-LD helps Bing and Copilot understand your content correctly. See our Schema.org guide.

Microsoft's AI Ecosystem: More Than One Bot

Copilot runs not only on the web but also in Windows 11, Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, Outlook) and the Edge browser. This integration multiplies the value of being visible on Bing — because most enterprise users live in the Microsoft ecosystem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Bing Copilot and ChatGPT the same?
No. Copilot is Microsoft's assistant using the Bing infrastructure; ChatGPT belongs to OpenAI. Even though Copilot uses OpenAI models in some versions, Bing indexing is essential for visibility.
What happens if I block bingbot?
Your site won't be indexed on Bing and Copilot can't cite you. You'll also lose your organic traffic from Bing search.
Do I need an extra file for Copilot visibility?
No separate file needed. HTTPS, open bingbot access, sitemap, canonical, meta description and structured data are enough.
