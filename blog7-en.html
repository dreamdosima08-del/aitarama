Perplexity SEO:
How to Rank on AI Search

📅 June 13, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 6 min read

Perplexity is one of the fastest-growing AI search engines of 2026. With over 100 million monthly queries, it has become a serious competitor to Google — and it evaluates websites very differently from traditional search engines. In this guide we explain step by step what you need to do to make your site visible on Perplexity.

Why Perplexity matters: On Perplexity, users get direct answers — and they're more likely to click through to your source site than on Google. Because Perplexity explicitly shows its sources and users follow them for more detail.

How Does Perplexity Work?

Perplexity crawls the web in real time for every query. Unlike Google, it doesn't rely on a pre-indexed database — it depends on live web crawling. This makes fresh and accessible content critically important.

Perplexity SEO Optimization — 6 Steps

1. Don't Block PerplexityBot

Explicitly allow PerplexityBot in your robots.txt file. Many sites unknowingly block this bot — our GPTBot check guide explains how to verify all AI bots.

robots.txt — Allow PerplexityBot
User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /

2. Write Content That Directly Answers Questions

Perplexity favors content that gives the clearest, most direct answer to a question. Your page's first paragraph should answer the main question in 2-3 sentences. Long introductions, storytelling or preambles are a disadvantage on Perplexity.

3. Keep Your Dates Current

Since Perplexity does real-time web crawling, it prefers fresh content. Update the dateModified and datePublished fields in your JSON-LD schema after every update.

4. Add Your llms.txt File

Perplexity has started supporting llms.txt files. This file provides context about your site. See our free llms.txt template guide to get started.

5. Create Citable Content

Perplexity includes specific data, statistics and definitions in its answers. Include these in your content: clear definitions, current statistics, step-by-step lists, comparison tables. This format increases the chance of Perplexity selecting your content as a source.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need to rank on Google to appear on Perplexity?
No. Perplexity does real-time web crawling and is not dependent on the Google index. Your content can be selected as a Perplexity source even if it doesn't rank on Google.
How long does it take to appear as a source on Perplexity?
After allowing PerplexityBot and updating content, you can typically get on Perplexity's radar within 1-2 weeks.
What languages does Perplexity support?
Perplexity supports over 30 languages. English content can be sourced for global queries while content in other languages is used for regional queries.
