Measuring AI Traffic: How Many Come from ChatGPT?

📅 June 22, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 7 min read

You've made your site AI-ready. But what next? How do you know when ChatGPT, Perplexity or Copilot cites you? Most site owners see AI-driven visitors mixed in with normal traffic and never even notice. This guide explains how to detect and measure AI-sourced traffic — because you can't improve what you can't measure.

Core idea: When AI engines send users to your site, those visits leave traces in your analytics. If you know where to look, you can answer the question "how many came from ChatGPT?"

What is AI Traffic?

AI traffic is when a user reaches your site by clicking a source link in an AI answer from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot or Google AI Overview. This differs from classic search traffic — the user has already read an AI answer and then came to your site for detail. So they're a more informed, more intentional visitor.

How Do You Detect AI Traffic?

1. Look at Referrer Sources

In Google Analytics or a similar tool, look for these domains among your referrer sources:

SourceReferrer Domain
ChatGPTchat.openai.com, chatgpt.com
Perplexityperplexity.ai
Copilotcopilot.microsoft.com, bing.com
Geminigemini.google.com

2. Analyze Traffic Behavior

AI-sourced visitors usually stay longer and browse deeper — because they're already interested in the topic. This behavioral difference is another way to recognize AI traffic.

3. UTM Tags Are Limited

AI engines usually don't add UTM parameters, so detection mostly relies on the referrer domain. That makes it a bit harder but not impossible.

Why Can't Most Sites See AI Traffic?

Two main reasons: first, most people don't know where to look. Second, some AI engines may hide referral information. But as your citation rate rises, this traffic becomes visible in your analytics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I see AI traffic in Google Analytics?
Yes, partially. You can detect AI-sourced visits by searching for domains like chatgpt.com and perplexity.ai among your referrer sources. However, not all AI engines send referral information.
Is AI traffic different from normal traffic?
Usually yes. Visitors from AI tend to be more intentional since they read an answer and came for detail; they tend to stay longer on the site.
What should I do to increase my AI traffic?
First solidify your AI visibility: bot access, llms.txt, structured data and citation readiness. As visibility grows, AI traffic grows too.
