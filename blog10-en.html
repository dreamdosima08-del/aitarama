Which Sites Does AI Trust? E-E-A-T

📅 June 16, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 7 min read

AI doesn't treat every site equally when generating an answer. It finds some sites trustworthy and cites them, while ignoring others entirely. So which sites does AI trust? The answer largely lies in the concept of E-E-A-T. This guide explains what E-E-A-T is and how to boost your site's credibility in the eyes of AI.

E-E-A-T stands for: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. AI prefers sites with strong signals across these four dimensions.

What is E-E-A-T?

E-E-A-T was originally a framework from Google's quality guidelines. But in the AI era, this concept has become far more critical — because AI models look for exactly these signals when deciding which content to trust.

Experience

Whether the content was written by someone who has genuinely experienced the topic. Content reflecting first-hand experience is more valuable to AI.

Expertise

Whether the person or organization producing the content is an expert on the subject. Author information, bylines and qualification signals matter here.

Authoritativeness

How well-known and referenced the site is in its field. Other trustworthy sites mentioning you generates authority.

Trustworthiness

The technical and content trustworthiness of the site: HTTPS, clear contact info, transparent author bylines, accurate and up-to-date information.

How Does AI Read E-E-A-T Signals?

AI evaluates E-E-A-T not as an abstract concept but through concrete technical signals:

E-E-A-T ComponentSignal AI Looks For
ExperienceFirst-hand examples, original content
ExpertiseAuthor byline, author schema
AuthoritativenessStructured data, external references
TrustworthinessHTTPS, contact page, fresh dates

How to Boost Your Site's AI Trust

1. Add Author Information

Add an author name, short bio and ideally author schema to each piece of content. This strengthens the expertise signal.

2. Use Structured Data

Organization and Article schema clearly tell AI your identity and authority. See our Schema.org guide.

3. Establish Technical Trust

Use HTTPS, clearly show your contact info, keep your content fresh. These are trustworthiness signals.

4. Open AI Access

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is E-E-A-T important for AI?
AI looks for experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness signals when deciding which content to trust. Sites with strong E-E-A-T are cited more often.
How do I improve my E-E-A-T score?
Add author information, use structured data, switch to HTTPS, show contact details, and keep your content up to date.
Can a small site achieve E-E-A-T?
Yes. With clear author bylines, accurate information and technical trustworthiness, small sites can produce strong E-E-A-T signals too.
