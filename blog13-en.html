How to Appear in Claude?

📅 June 25, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 6 min read

While ChatGPT and Gemini get all the attention, a powerful AI assistant often goes unnoticed: Claude. Built by Anthropic, Claude is spreading fast, especially in the enterprise world and among developers. So can Claude see your site? This guide explains how Claude reads the web and how to open your site to it.

Why it matters: Claude excels at understanding long, complex content. It's frequently chosen for technical, legal and academic content. If your site covers these areas, Claude visibility is critical.

How Does Claude Read the Web?

Claude has two main access methods: ClaudeBot (the content crawler) and Claude-User (user-triggered access). The first lets Anthropic gather content, the second activates when a user gives Claude a link.

Check ClaudeBot Access

If your robots.txt blocks ClaudeBot, Claude can't read your site or cite it. Our robots.txt check guide explains how to tell whether it's blocked.

Correct robots.txt Structure

There should be no Disallow: / rule for ClaudeBot and Claude-User. The ideal is a clean structure open to all AI bots.

How to Optimize Content for Claude?

1. Clear and Structured Content

Claude loves well-structured content broken into headings. Even long texts are understood better when split into logical sections.

2. Context and Depth

Claude values content that covers a topic in depth over shallow content. E-E-A-T signals apply here too.

3. Structured Data

Schema.org and JSON-LD let Claude understand exactly what your content is. See our Schema.org guide.

Is Claude Different From Other Engines?

Core GEO principles (bot access, structured data, clear content) are shared across all engines. But Claude's strength is understanding long context — so comprehensive, well-written content gives an extra advantage with Claude. Lay the foundation with our GEO guide.

What happens if I block ClaudeBot?
Claude can't read your site and can't cite it when users ask Claude. You lose your visibility.
