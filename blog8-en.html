What is GEO? Generative Engine Optimization Guide

📅 June 16, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 7 min read

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the discipline of optimizing your website for generative AI engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. As of 2026, a significant share of searches now flows through AI answers rather than the traditional Google results page. This guide explains what GEO is, how it differs from classic SEO, and how to make your site GEO-ready step by step.

Quick definition: SEO optimizes your site for search engines, while GEO optimizes it for AI engines. They complement each other but operate by different rules.

What is GEO?

GEO stands for "Generative Engine Optimization." In traditional SEO, the goal was ranking high on Google. In GEO, the goal is for your site to be cited as a source and your content used in the answer when a user asks an AI a question.

This difference is critical: even if you rank #1 on Google, if ChatGPT doesn't know you, you're invisible in the AI era. GEO fills exactly this gap.

Why is GEO Critical in 2026?

Search Behavior Has Changed

Instead of typing "best coffee maker" and scanning ten blue links, users now ask AI directly and get a single synthesized answer. If you're not in that answer, you lose a major share of your traffic.

AI Shows Sources

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overview display source links beneath their answers. Being among these sources is the new-generation equivalent of ranking first on Google.

5 Core Steps to Make Your Site GEO-Ready

1. Allow AI Bots

Make sure your robots.txt doesn't block GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot. We covered how in our GPTBot check guide (official OpenAI GPTBot docs).

2. Add llms.txt

llms.txt is a new standard that gives AI a summary of your site. Use our free llms.txt template.

3. Use Structured Data

Schema.org and JSON-LD help AI understand your content. Check our Schema.org guide.

4. Write in Q&A Format

AI looks for clear answers to clear questions. Structure your content with question headings and concise answers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are GEO and SEO the same thing?
No. SEO optimizes your site for search engines, while GEO optimizes it for AI engines. GEO doesn't replace SEO; it carries it into the AI era.
Do I need to know code for GEO?
No. Basic steps like adding llms.txt and editing robots.txt require no coding. Tools like Scanliq provide ready-made fixes.
How long until GEO results show?
It usually takes 1-4 weeks for AI bots to re-crawl your site and process changes.
