What is AI Content Analysis?

AI content analysis evaluates how suitable an article or text is to be cited by AI systems. Engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI prefer clear, specific and well-structured content when generating answers. This tool measures how well your text meets these criteria.

Why is Citation Readiness Important?

AI cites sources when generating an answer. If your content is not ready to be cited, you stay invisible while competitors get cited. High citation-readiness content is critical for both appearing in AI answers and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) success.
