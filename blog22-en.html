How to Build E-E-A-T Trust Signals

📅 July 3, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 8 min read

Why doesn't AI cite your site? Not because your content is bad — usually because your trust signals are missing. Systems like Google AI Overview and ChatGPT want to trust a source before recommending it. That trust has a name: E-E-A-T. This guide explains what E-E-A-T means in the AI era and how to build the trust signals needed to appear in AI Overview.

Critical data: Pages with strong E-E-A-T signals are more than 2x as likely to be cited in AI Overview. Google's own spokesperson confirmed it: "SEO for AI is still SEO." The same trust signals determine both your ranking and your AI citations.

What is E-E-A-T?

E-E-A-T is a four-letter framework Google uses to evaluate content quality: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. It started as E-A-T; in 2022 Google added the first "E" (Experience). We covered the fundamentals in our E-E-A-T guide — this post focuses on how to build these signals step by step, especially to get cited in AI Overview and ChatGPT answers.

E-E-A-T and AI Overview: The Connection

Here's the most important truth of 2026: Google AI Overview draws from the same index that E-E-A-T standards govern. So E-E-A-T is no longer just for ranking — it's the filter that determines whether AI cites you. Without strong E-E-A-T, AI won't include you in its answer.

Research shows it clearly: the vast majority of companies invest in traditional SEO yet stay invisible in AI. The reason isn't bad content — it's that they haven't built the trust signals. This is exactly one of the most common mistakes.

How to Build E-E-A-T Signals for AI?

1. Experience: Show Real Proof

AI looks for concrete proof instead of generic statements like "improves sales." Specific, verifiable data like "increased demo bookings by 340% in 8 weeks" is the kind of content AI cites. Real case studies, original data and first-hand experience are your power source that AI cannot replicate.

2. Expertise: Build Author Identity

In 2026, author entity isn't optional. Add named, credentialed expert authors to your content. Consistent identity and Person Schema build the "author vector" Google uses to evaluate your content's trustworthiness.

3. Authoritativeness: Build a Topic Cluster

Identify 3-5 core topics your brand should be authoritative on. Create a comprehensive pillar page for each, supported by deep sub-content. Interlinking these signals deep expertise. Third-party mentions (other trusted sites referring to you) — even without links — build trust.

4. Trustworthiness: Be Transparent

HTTPS, clear About and Contact pages, privacy policy, correction policies and current content. AI prefers verifiable, transparent sources. Even recency is a measurable trust signal — use current dates and dateModified.

Structured Data: Tell Machines About Your Trust

Google is clear: use accurate Schema markup that aligns with your page content. Mismatches between structured data and visible content undermine trust signals. See our Schema.org guide to set up Organization, Article and Person schema correctly.

E-E-A-T Isn't a One-Time Task

E-E-A-T isn't a checkbox but a continuous investment. In 2026, as AI content floods the web, the gap between sites with strong E-E-A-T and those without keeps widening. Businesses that treat it as an ongoing investment hold positions competitors will struggle to challenge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is E-E-A-T a ranking factor?
Not a direct ranking factor — it's a framework Google's human quality raters use. But the signals that demonstrate E-E-A-T (author reputation, content depth, accuracy, trustworthiness) are measurable and directly influence both ranking and AI citation.
Why is E-E-A-T important for appearing in AI Overview?
Because Google AI Overview draws from the same index that E-E-A-T standards govern. Strong E-E-A-T makes you more than 2x as likely to be cited in AI Overview.
Can a new site build E-E-A-T?
Yes. You can start by focusing on a narrow topic, showing first-hand experience and being transparent. E-E-A-T builds momentum over time — not overnight, but through consistent investment.
