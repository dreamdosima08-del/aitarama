7 Most Common AI Visibility Mistakes and Fixes

📅 June 28, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 8 min read

Are you working hard to prepare your site for AI but getting no results? The problem is usually small but critical mistakes that go unnoticed. Here are the 7 most common mistakes that sabotage AI visibility — and the fix for each. Check your site against these mistakes.

Note: Most of these mistakes look small individually, but combined they can make your site completely invisible to AI engines. The good news: all are fixable.

Mistake 1: Blocking AI Bots

The most common and most fatal mistake. If your robots.txt blocks GPTBot, ClaudeBot or PerplexityBot, AI can't see your site even if you do everything else perfectly. Check your robots.txt.

Mistake 2: No llms.txt File

llms.txt is the new standard that introduces your site to AI. Not having it means missing the chance to present yourself to AI. Add it in minutes with our free template.

Mistake 3: Missing Structured Data

Without Schema.org and JSON-LD, AI has to guess what your content is about. Structured data tells AI clearly. See our Schema guide.

Mistake 4: Content Buried in JavaScript

If your content loads via JavaScript, most AI bots can't see it. A page that looks great in the browser can be empty for AI. Read about the ghost page problem.

Mistake 5: Weak, Generic Content

Generic phrases like "quality service, fair prices" are worthless to AI. AI cites specific, verifiable, clear info. Learn the AI citation logic.

Mistake 6: Focusing on One Engine

Thinking only about ChatGPT and neglecting Google AI, Perplexity and Copilot means missing a large share of your audience. Target all four engines.

Mistake 7: Doing It Once and Forgetting

AI visibility isn't static. Algorithms change, your content ages, competitors improve. If you don't check regularly, you'll lose the visibility you gained. Apply our checklist regularly.
