GEO vs SEO: What's the Difference?

📅 July 1, 2026✍️ Scanliq Team⏱️ 8 min read

"Is SEO dead?" "Should we do GEO now?" These questions are on every digital marketer's mind in 2026. The short answer: SEO isn't dead, but it's no longer enough on its own. GEO doesn't replace SEO — it adds a new layer on top. This guide clearly explains the difference between GEO and SEO and when to focus on each.

The difference in one sentence: SEO focuses on ranking your site on Google (goal: a click). GEO focuses on getting AI to cite you (goal: appearing in the AI answer).

What is SEO? (Quick Reminder)

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the practice of ranking your website higher in traditional search engines like Google and Bing. It's built on keywords, backlinks, technical structure and authority signals. The goal is clear: appear at the top of a list of blue links and get the user to click yours.

What is GEO? (The New Layer)

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring your content so AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI cite it. The difference from traditional search: AI doesn't offer a list of links — it gathers information from multiple sources and generates a single answer. Your job is to be in that answer. SEO vs GEO: Key Differences

User behaviorClicks a linkReads the answer (often no click)
Core signalKeywords, backlinksStructure, clarity, authority, citability

Why Are Both Needed?

Here's the critical point: GEO and SEO aren't competitors, they're complementary. Research shows AI answers are mostly fed by content that already ranks well on Google. So a strong SEO foundation also sets the stage for GEO success. Content Google finds high-quality is often the content AI trusts too.

But if you only do SEO, you miss zero-click searches and AI visibility. If you focus only on GEO, you lose traffic from traditional search. That's exactly one of the most common mistakes.

Why Does This Shift Matter Now?

The numbers make it clear: research shows a significant share of Google searches now end without a click — users get the answer directly on the search page. At the same time, millions of users turn straight to AI for answers. This means ranking on Google alone is no longer enough.

When Should You Focus on What?

SEO Foundation First

Technical health, a fast site, quality content and backlinks — these are the foundation for both SEO and GEO. Our AI SEO checklist helps you build this foundation.

Then the GEO Layer

Bot access (robots.txt, llms.txt), structured data, clear and citable content. These make you visible in AI answers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SEO dead, should I only do GEO now?
No, SEO isn't dead. GEO doesn't replace it, it adds a new layer. Even AI answers are mostly fed by content that ranks well on Google, so a strong SEO foundation is also needed for GEO.
What's the most fundamental difference between GEO and SEO?
SEO focuses on ranking your site in search engines (click goal). GEO focuses on getting AI to cite you (appearing in the AI answer).
Can a small business do both SEO and GEO?
Yes. The good news is they overlap heavily — producing quality, clear, structured content serves both. Build the SEO foundation first, then add the GEO layer.
